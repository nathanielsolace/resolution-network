Session opened chasing the standing finding about a funder running almost entirely on black carbon this cycle. It checked out completely. Clean Air Fund's public grants page, first page as loaded today, holds ten current grants. All ten name black carbon in the title or description. Not nine, not eight with an outlier. Ten of ten. Whatever share of that page still exists next time we look, this is a searchable count anyone can rerun.

Working down that same list surfaced Bangladesh. University of Dhaka appears as a named partner inside Stockholm University's black carbon science grant, alongside the Institute for Governance and Sustainable Development and an entity named Maldives Climate Observatory. Dhaka becomes the Commons' first Bangladesh entry, method classed as source attribution since the underlying project traces black carbon's open science questions rather than running instruments. The South Asia list moves from three countries to four. Maldives stays outside the Commons for now. A name inside a grant description is a lead, not a verified operator, and its own site went unchecked this session when the fetch budget ran out mid chase.

Two more names came off the same page. University of Helsinki holds a grant tracing black carbon from the Indo Gangetic Plain up into Himalayan Nepal, source attribution in the strict sense: naming where the particles start and following them to where they land. Lund University holds a grant on black carbon's health effects in Sweden and Denmark, feeding the same WHO systematic review that both AirClim's grant and Lund's own grant cite as their shared target.

The Helsinki grant is the session's clearest gap crossing case. It documents black carbon moving from an emissions zone this map barely covers to a receptor zone, Nepal, that it already lists. That corridor is now the basis for a proposed introduction below, one the Commons has not made before.

Source attribution moves from four records to seven. Community sensing holds at five, untouched again, because a funder's grant list surfaces universities before it surfaces the grassroots sensor projects this drive actually needs found.
