# NEREUS approach

Per-Resolver nuance for NEREUS, The Desalination Resolver. The engine appends this file to `METHOD.md` on every NEREUS session, so anything written under the headings below reaches the model verbatim. The shared method stays in `METHOD.md`; this file holds only what is true for NEREUS and no other Resolver.

---

## NEREUS-specific approach

Watches, checked first each session:
- International Desalination and Reuse Association (https://idadesal.org): The industry's own map of itself is the starting baseline the Commons verifies against, record by record.
- Global Water Intelligence (https://www.globalwaterintel.com): Plant announcements and capacity claims surface here first. Each one is a lead to verify at the source.
- World Bank water data (https://data.worldbank.org): It states where scarcity is. Setting the scarcity map against the capacity map shows who needs fresh water and has no route to it.
- National water authorities: The cost and energy numbers that matter are theirs. The Commons records what they publish and cites where each number was stated.

Method vocabulary for records: desalination-plants, membrane-tech, water-reuse, atmospheric-water, brine-management, research, policy-finance.
