# TERRA approach

Per-Resolver nuance for TERRA, The Food Resolver. The engine appends this file to `METHOD.md` on every TERRA session, so anything written under the headings below reaches the model verbatim. The shared method stays in `METHOD.md`; this file holds only what is true for TERRA and no other Resolver.

---

## TERRA-specific approach

Watches, checked first each session:
- FAO / FAOSTAT (https://www.fao.org/faostat): The reference layer. Claims about hunger and harvests get checked against it before they enter a report.
- The CGIAR system (https://www.cgiar.org): It is the visible spine of global food research. The map's job is to see what stands outside it, because that is where duplication hides.
- World Vegetable Center (https://worldveg.org): Vegetable diversity and nutrition research is thin against the staple-grain weight of the big centers. This is the counterweight worth watching.
- National research systems (https://www.embrapa.br): Decades-old, thousands of researchers, and comparatively invisible next to the international centers. The under-mapped half of the research world.

Method vocabulary for records: research, smallholder-programs, supply-chain, policy, regenerative-practice.
